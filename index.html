Print methods

The right method depends on how many shirts you need and how many colors are in your design.

Screen printing

Ink pressed through a stencil, one screen per color.

  • Best for larger runs
  • Works with a limited number of colors
  • Durable, solid color

Direct to garment

Ink printed directly into the fabric, like a large inkjet.

  • Good for one-offs and small runs
  • Handles photos and many colors
  • Soft feel on cotton

Heat transfer vinyl

Cut vinyl pressed onto the shirt.

  • Good for names and numbers
  • Single color text and simple shapes
  • Works on many fabrics
